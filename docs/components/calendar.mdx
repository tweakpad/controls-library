## Usage

Values are `YYYY-MM-DD` strings with no time zone, so a selected 20th never becomes the 19th. The Calendar has no surface of its own; place it in a Card, Popover or page section that provides one. Day and navigation controls are actual Button components; caption selectors are actual Native Select components. Add a border or fill on the host, or style the `calendar` part.

### Range

`selection-mode="range"` starts a range on the first activation and completes it on the second. As an attribute, a range is written `from/to`. `visible-months` shows consecutive months side by side. A range with only `from` is incomplete; its anchor is both range start and range end.

<Preview
  title="Range"
  code={`<tp-card section-colors="off" style="inline-size:fit-content">
  <tp-calendar
    label="Stay"
    selection-mode="range"
    visible-months="2"
    today="2026-01-05"
    default-value="2026-01-12/2026-01-19"
  ></tp-calendar>
</tp-card>`}
/>

`minimum-nights` and `maximum-nights` limit the range length; `range-exclusion` decides whether a range across a disabled date is rejected or restarted.

### Multiple dates

In `multiple` mode each activation toggles one date. Write the attribute value as space-separated dates, and cap the selection with `maximum-selection-count`.

    default-value="2026-06-03 2026-06-10 2026-06-17"

</tp-card>`}
/>

### Bounds and unavailable dates

`min` and `max` disable dates outside the selection window while still displaying them. For specific dates, assign a `Set` or predicate to the `disabledDates` or `unavailableDates` property.

<Preview
  title="Bookable dates"
  code={`<tp-card section-colors="off" style="inline-size:fit-content">
  <tp-calendar
    id="calendar-bookable"
    label="Appointment"
    today="2026-06-08"
    min="2026-06-08"
    max="2026-06-26"
  ></tp-calendar>
</tp-card>`}
  script={`const calendar = document.getElementById('calendar-bookable');
await customElements.whenDefined('tp-calendar');
// Weekends are closed; two days are already fully booked.
const booked = new Set(['2026-06-11', '2026-06-18']);
calendar.unavailableDates = (date) => booked.has(date);
calendar.disabledDates = (date) => [0, 6].includes(new Date(date + 'T00:00').getDay());`}
/>

Disabled and unavailable dates are skipped by keyboard movement and cannot be selected; unavailable ones carry a separate `data-unavailable` marker for styling. Unavailable dates are crossed out at full strength; disabled dates are dimmed.

### Custom day content

<Preview
  title="Nightly prices and booked dates"
  code={`<tp-card section-colors="off" style="inline-size:fit-content">
  <tp-calendar
    id="calendar-prices"
    label="Stay"
    selection-mode="range"
    today="2026-12-01"
    default-value="2026-12-08/2026-12-11"
    style="--tp-calendar-cell-size:calc(var(--tp-spacing) * 14)"
  ></tp-calendar>
</tp-card>`}
  script={`import { html } from 'lit';

await customElements.whenDefined('tp-calendar');
const calendar = document.getElementById('calendar-prices');
const booked = new Set(['2026-12-17', '2026-12-18', '2026-12-19', '2026-12-20']);
const weekend = (date) => [5, 6].includes(new Date(date + 'T00:00').getDay());
calendar.disabledDates = booked;
calendar.dayModifiers = { booked };
calendar.messages = {
  day: (day) => (day.modifiers.booked ? day.label + ', booked' : day.label),
};
calendar.renderDay = (day) => {
  const number = Number(day.date.slice(8));
  if (day.modifiers.booked) return html\`<s>\${number}</s>\`;
  if (day.outside) return undefined;
  const price = weekend(day.date) ? '$180' : '$140';
  return html\`<tp-stack style="line-height:1.1">\${number}<small style="font-size:var(--tp-text-xs);opacity:0.7">\${price}</small></tp-stack>\`;
};`}
/>

`renderDay` puts your own content inside each day button, here a nightly rate, while the button keeps its date name, focus and selection. Keep that content passive. `dayModifiers` tags dates with a `data-booked` marker and `day.modifiers.booked`, so the struck-through days can also say "booked" to screen readers through `messages.day`. `--tp-calendar-cell-size` makes room for the extra line.

The day Button is the only activation and focus target, so custom content cannot add interactive descendants: links, buttons, form controls, library controls, `tabindex` or editable elements, and interactive ARIA roles are unsupported. Such content produces a `calendar-interactive-day-content` diagnostic. Modifier names must be lowercase (`a-z`, `0-9`, `-`).

`--tp-calendar-cell-size` sets the size of every day, weekday, week-number and navigation cell. Increase it for custom day content, responsively if needed:

<Code code={`tp-calendar.roomy {
  --tp-calendar-cell-size: calc(var(--tp-spacing) * 12);
}
@media (min-width: 48rem) {
  tp-calendar.roomy {
    --tp-calendar-cell-size: calc(var(--tp-spacing) * 14);
  }
}`} language="css" />

When a weekday abbreviation does not fit its cell, as in a compact density region or with a small cell size, every weekday shows its narrow form, usually its first letter; the column keeps the abbreviation as its name.

### Month and year selectors

`caption-layout="dropdown"` replaces the caption text with native selects, which helps when the date may be years away, such as a birth date. Use `dropdown-months` or `dropdown-years` for one selector.

Without navigation bounds, the year dropdown offers the current year minus 100 through the current year. That list does not restrict selection. With navigation bounds, a month choice is disabled when that month cannot be displayed, and a year choice is disabled when none of its months can be; the displayed year always stays available. Choosing a year keeps the displayed month when it can be shown; if that month of the chosen year is outside the bounds, the change lands on the nearest month of that year that can be shown. Either way it is a normal `tp-displayed-month-change` proposal.

### Week numbers and footer

`show-week-number` adds a locale-aware week column, `fixed-weeks` keeps six rows so the height never jumps, and the `footer` slot adds content below the months. Empty footers take no space. Custom adapters must implement weekNumber.

  <tp-stack slot="footer" direction="row" align="center" justify="space-between" gap="2" style="inline-size: 100%">

    <tp-button variant="ghost" size="sm">Remove date</tp-button>
  </tp-stack>

  script={`await customElements.whenDefined('tp-calendar');
const card = document.getElementById('calendar-due');
const calendar = card.querySelector('tp-calendar');
const picks = card.querySelector('tp-toggle-group');
const summary = card.querySelector('output');
const iso = (date) =>
  [date.getFullYear(), date.getMonth() + 1, date.getDate()]
    .map((part) => String(part).padStart(2, '0'))
    .join('-');
// Each quick pick names a date relative to today.
const pickDate = (pick) => {
  const date = new Date(calendar.today + 'T00:00');
  const days = pick === 'monday' ? (8 - date.getDay()) % 7 || 7 : Number(pick);
  date.setDate(date.getDate() + days);
  return iso(date);
};
const show = (value) => {
  calendar.value = value;
  const match = [...picks.querySelectorAll('tp-toggle')].find((pick) => pickDate(pick.value) === value);
  picks.value = match ? [match.value] : [];

    ? 'Due ' + new Date(value + 'T00:00').toLocaleDateString(undefined, { weekday: 'long', day: 'numeric', month: 'long' })
    : 'No due date';
};
calendar.addEventListener('tp-value-change', (event) => show(event.detail.value));
// A pick applies on every press, so pressing the current pick again brings its date back into view.
picks.addEventListener('click', (event) => {
  const pick = event.target.closest('tp-toggle');
  if (pick) show(pickDate(pick.value));
});
card.querySelector('tp-button').addEventListener('click', () => show(null));
show(calendar.value);`}
/>

Quick picks and the Calendar drive one controlled date: a pick writes `value`, which brings the date into view even when it is already selected, and a date picked on the grid selects the matching pick or none. `fixed-weeks` keeps the card from changing height between months.

### Locale

`locale` takes a BCP 47 tag and formats the caption, weekday names, day numbers and accessible date names through `Intl`. Without it the Calendar follows the document language, then the browser. The first day of the week also comes from the locale, Monday in French and Sunday in Brazilian Portuguese, unless `week-starts-on` sets it.

<Preview
  title="French and Brazilian Portuguese"
  code={`<tp-stack direction="row" gap="4" justify="center" wrap="wrap">
  <tp-card section-colors="off" style="inline-size: fit-content">
    <tp-calendar
      id="calendar-locale-fr"
      label="Date de livraison"
      locale="fr"
      today="2026-06-08"
      default-value="2026-06-12"
    ></tp-calendar>
  </tp-card>
  <tp-card section-colors="off" style="inline-size: fit-content">
    <tp-calendar
      label="Data de entrega"
      locale="pt-BR"
      today="2026-06-08"
      default-value="2026-06-12"
    ></tp-calendar>
  </tp-card>
</tp-stack>`}
  script={`await customElements.whenDefined('tp-calendar');
document.getElementById('calendar-locale-fr').messages = {
  previous: (_month, caption) => 'Mois précédent, ' + caption,

The value stays a `YYYY-MM-DD` string in every locale. `label` names the group; the remaining control names and validation messages come from the `messages` property, which falls back to English. The French calendar above names its navigation buttons that way.

`month` is the first day of the month the control navigates to, and `caption` its locale-formatted name. `CalendarValidityCode` is `value-missing`, `excluded-date`, `selection-count`, `range-excluded-date` or `range-nights`; the message is reported through `checkValidity()`, `reportValidity()` and Field. Diagnostics from `tp-diagnostic` are developer-facing and stay in English.

### Forms and events

With a `name`, the Calendar submits its value and takes part in validation; `required` reports a missing date through Field. Each selection emits a cancelable `tp-value-change` whose `detail.value` holds the new selection. The form value is the single date, one repeated entry per date in multiple mode, or `from` and `to` entries for a range. Reset restores `defaultValue`. Required reports `valueMissing` (message `messages.validity('value-missing')`) with no selection and prevents clearing a non-empty selection. Read-only keeps navigation and focus but rejects selection. Disabled removes the calendar from focus and submission.

For a controlled Calendar, set `value` and assign the proposed value synchronously in the `tp-value-change` listener; leaving it unchanged rejects the change. Canceling the event rejects it in either mode. Programmatic and controlled values are validated against the same bounds, exclusions, counts and night limits as activation.

### Keyboard

One day is in the tab order. Arrow keys move by day and week, Home and End to the week's edges, Page Up and Page Down by month, and Shift with Page Up or Page Down by year. Enter and Space select the focused day. Movement skips disabled and hidden dates and reveals other months within the navigation bounds. Consumed keys and keys carrying Alt, Ctrl or Meta are left to the browser.

### Day strip

`variant="strip"` replaces the month grid with a row of consecutive days under the month caption, for picking a day close to today.

<Preview
  title="Day strip"
  code={`<tp-card section-colors="off" style="inline-size:fit-content">
  <tp-calendar label="Pickup day" name="pickup" variant="strip" today="2026-06-29" default-value="2026-07-01"></tp-calendar>
</tp-card>`}
/>

A single selection stays in the middle of the strip: the arrows beside the days select the previous or next day, skipping days that cannot be selected, and a day pressed at either side slides to the middle. With a range, several dates or `readonly`, the arrows slide the days a day at a time instead, or a whole row with `paged-navigation`. `visible-days` sets how many days show. Longer jumps fade the new days in, and reduced motion or `motion-policy="reduce"` moves them at once. The caption names the month of the middle day; its arrows move to the same day of the previous or next month. Selection modes, bounds and custom day content work as they do in the grid. From the keyboard, Home and End reach the first and last day shown, and moving focus past either end brings the days along.

`crossedOutDates` crosses out days that can still be selected, here full classes that take a waitlist. Unavailable dates are crossed out and cannot be selected, and disabled dates, such as the days before `min`, are dimmed. A day both crossed out and disabled is crossed out and dimmed.

`dayMarkers` puts up to three dots under a day's number, as a `Map` or a function from the date to a list of markers. Each marker takes a Status dot `variant` or any CSS `color`, and its `label` is read after the date. `today-indicator` marks today with the muted fill (`fill`, the default), an underlined number (`underline`), a dot (`dot`), or not at all (`none`).
