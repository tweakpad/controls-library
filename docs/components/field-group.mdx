## Usage

Field group lays out related editors and joins their borders. It owns only the layout, the seams and the group name: each editor keeps its own value, `name`, label, state and validation, and the group submits nothing itself. `label` names the tuple ("Canvas size"); every editor still needs its own complete `label`, because prefixes like `W` are visual shorthand.

It is the editor counterpart of [Button group](../button-group/) and shares its seam treatment.

To stack separate Fields with spacing, use Field's `group` slot instead.

### Tuples

Editors share the main axis equally. Keep a related value that is not part of the tuple, such as opacity, outside the group so it reads as separate.

An editor that declares its own `inline-size` or `flex` keeps it. Inside a container with a definite inline size the editors shrink below their intrinsic width; an ancestor that is sized by its content (a grid auto track, `fit-content`) keeps each native input's intrinsic minimum, so give such containers an inline size.

### Mixed members

Buttons and Toggles can join beside the editors. A Separator divides editors and takes the perpendicular orientation automatically.

Input, Input group, Text area, Select and Native select are editor members. Button and Toggle can join beside them, and Menu or Popover participate through their slotted trigger; popup content is never a member.

Hidden members (`hidden`) do not participate. Reordering, removing or replacing a member updates the seams and part registrations without replacing other controls. Content that is not a member splits the group into independent seam runs.

  <tp-input-group><span slot="prefix">$</span><tp-input label="Minimum price" default-value="20"></tp-input></tp-input-group>
  <tp-separator></tp-separator>

</tp-field-group>`}

### Member states

Each editor keeps its own state inside the group. A read-only or disabled editor shows its usual treatment while its neighbours stay editable.

Disable or make read-only the editors individually (`disabled`, `readonly`), or wrap the group in a disabled fieldset through Field. The group adds no selection state: it never implies that one member is chosen.

### Orientation

`orientation="vertical"` stacks the editors, stretches them to the widest one and joins their block edges. Use it for long tuples that would become unreadable in one row.

<Preview
  title="Vertical"
  align="stretch"

  <tp-input label="Street address" placeholder="Street address" default-value="221 Market Street"></tp-input>

### Inline

<Preview

  align="stretch"

    <tp-input-group><span slot="prefix">Z</span><tp-input label="Rotation Z" type="number" min="-180" max="180" default-value="90"></tp-input></tp-input-group>
  </tp-field-group>

/>

`variant="inline"` fits the group into one row the height of a control, with `label` at the start and the values at the end, so it stacks with other inline controls such as an inline Input. Each editor becomes a segment of the row: its prefix, then its value, as wide as its text. The row carries the boundary, the focus ring and the invalid state, and a press on the label edits the first value.

Number values step with the Arrow keys, ten steps at a time with Shift, and keep to their `step`, `min` and `max`; a committed value rounds to the decimals of `step`. A value off the step or out of range shows the whole row invalid, and a long value scrolls inside its own segment.

### In widgets

The color picker composes a Field group for its channel editors (R/G/B, H/S/L, …, or the single hex editor) and keeps the alpha editor apart with the row gap, so every format reads as one tuple plus opacity.
