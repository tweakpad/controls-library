Calendar fills a wider inline size: months and columns share the extra width while rows keep the cell height and days keep the cell size. Writing `value`, including the selected value again, brings the selection into view through the displayed-month proposal. Scroll Area tracks now show only while scrolling by default (`scrollbar-visibility="while-scrolling"`). A Stack keeps filling its host when a layout such as Form actions makes the host a flex container.
