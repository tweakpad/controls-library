New widget: Heatmap (`tp-heatmap`, from `@tweakpad/ui/widgets`), values as a grid of colored cells such as activity over a year. Each datum is `{ key, value }` (optional `level`, `label` and a `hint` shown in the tooltip); `unit` sets the keys (`day`, `hour`, `week`, `month`, `year`, `index` or `custom`) and `columns` and `rows` project them onto the grid: built-in date projections (`week`, `month`, `weekday`, `day-of-month`, `hour-of-day`…), a wrap count, or a custom axis. A cell exists only where a key of the `start`–`end` range lands, and keys landing on one cell combine by `aggregate`. `levels`, `scale` (`linear`, `quantile`), `thresholds`, `domain`, `color` (a theme color or any CSS color) and `colors` set the ramp; `size` (`xs` to `lg`) and `shape` (`square`, `rounded`, `circle`) the cells; `fit` stretches the cells, scrolls them (opening at the latest data, with shaded edges) or trims the oldest columns. Labels follow the locale and week start; one Tooltip shows the hovered or focused cell; `selection-mode` reuses Calendar's single, multiple and range selection (`value`, `tp-value-change`, `setValue`); `tp-inspection-change` and `tp-cell-activate` report inspection and activation.

Calendar: its week start, month labels, week enumeration, date matchers, weekday label fitting and grid key handling now come from shared Foundation services that the heatmap uses too; `CalendarAdapter` gains an optional `monthLabel(date, locale, width)`, and the selection functions accept any ordered key domain.
