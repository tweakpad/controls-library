Add `variant="inline"` to Field group, placing its label and compound values in one compact row with a shared boundary, focus state, and validation state. Numeric members keep stepping, units, and their own form values. Inline groups can expand into individual editor rows with `expandable` and `expanded`, and `constraints` can keep related values proportional or apply a custom rule. Add an `icon` slot to inline controls and a `unit` property to inline Input.
