New widget: Layers (`tp-layers`, from `@tweakpad/ui/widgets`), a stack of layers people select, reorder, group, rename and change the states of, as in an image or design editor. Layers are declared as `tp-layer` children (an `id`, a `label`, a `tp-icon`, nested `tp-layer` children for a group) that the stack keeps in step with every accepted edit, or passed as records through `defaultLayers` or a controlled `layers`. Each layer declares its own states as `tp-layer-state` children (`name`, `label`, `value`, `scope`) with any number of `tp-layer-state-value` children (`value`, `label`, a `tp-icon`, `display="hover"`, `dims`); a press on a state's control proposes its next value. `enabled` is the one built-in state: a layer whose `enabled` is `false` is frozen. Several layers can be selected (Shift ranges, Control or Command toggles) and one of them is the active layer; `value` (`tp-value-change`) and `active` (`tp-active-change`) are proposed together, and every change is a cancelable `tp-layers-change` whose `metadata.change` names the action. `can-rename`, `can-remove` and `can-reorder` say what people may do, and a `tp-layer`'s own `can-rename` and `can-remove` override the stack's; F2 or a double-click renames, Delete removes and Alt+Arrow moves the selection; `addLayer`, `duplicateLayers`, `removeLayers`, `moveLayers`, `groupLayers`, `ungroupLayers`, `selectLayers`, `setLayerState` and `renameLayer` work from code; `header`, `footer` and `empty` slots hold the app's controls.

Tree view: `selection-behavior="replace"` makes presses and arrow keys select the item alone, with Control or Command toggling and Shift extending; selection proposals carry the item acted on in `metadata.item`; `isItemCurrent` marks a current item with `aria-current` and `data-current`.
