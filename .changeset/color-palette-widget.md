'@tweakpad/ui': minor

New Color palette widget (`tp-color-palette`): chooses a color from swatch collections of any size. The Colors tab shows a grid of rounded chips, gapless squares in a flat region (`appearance="flat"`), grouped by the collection or by hue and sorted by lightness, hue or chroma; the Palettes tab shows the collection's palettes of up to twelve colors, as hard stops or blended as gradients, sized by their weights. The Favorites tab lists bookmarked colors, palettes and gradients: double-click a color or a palette to add it, and remove one with a double-click, Delete or the context menu; each palette and gradient is listed under its name; favorites are kept in local storage by default (`persistence`, `storageKey`) or owned by the application through `favorites` and `tp-favorites-change`. The view options set the Colors layout (cells, or a list of rows with each color's name or HSL values), its groups (auto hue groups by lightness, the collection's groups, or none) and the cell size, which reflows the cells inside a box that only the density resizes; the list takes focus as one control with grid arrows, typeahead and Enter or Space; cells follow the size and the density of their region, and the chosen cell, or the whole palette of a color picked in one, is marked by a white and a black line along its edge. Collections are JSON documents with colors, groups and `palettes` (`parseColorCollection`, `colorCollectionSchema`); the Color collections submenu of the view options offers the default collection, then the loaded ones, and a status bar under the list shows the chosen color in `format` (or the chosen palette) with a copy button and a favorite heart, or the collection's name and size when nothing is chosen, and, with nothing chosen, an info icon that credits the active collection's source and license, in a Preview Card with the new `description` when the collection has one; `@tweakpad/ui/color-palette` ships the model with a default and a reduced collection, and every other collection is its own entry, `@tweakpad/ui/color-palette/collections/<id>` (the CSS named colors, the risograph inks with the Riso Club combinations as palettes, the ColorBrewer colors with every scheme size as palettes, the traditional colors of Japan from Nippon Colors, Sanzo Wada's color combinations, the film-inspired palettes of the wesanderson package, Le Corbusier's Polychromie Architecturale with selections from his 1931 color keyboards, the named colors of Werner's Nomenclature of Colours, a selection of Lospec pixel-art palettes named with their authors, and a selection of the nice-color-palettes package). A collection can also be a loader (`{ id, name, load }`) that the palette calls only when the collection becomes active: `colorCollectionCatalog` offers every shipped collection this way, the list shows a Spinner while one loads and the error with a Retry Button when it fails, and `collectionStatus`, the host's `data-status` and `tp-loading-status-change` report its state. The Color picker's swatches view now composes the palette and takes `collections`; the schemes view and the `schemes` property are removed, and the recent strip draws rounded chips, or gapless swatches with one edge in a flat region.
