New Color palette widget (`tp-color-palette`): chooses a color from swatch collections of any size. The Colors tab shows a gapless grid grouped by the collection or by hue and sorted by lightness, hue or chroma; the Palettes tab shows the collection's palettes of up to twelve colors, as hard stops or blended as gradients, sized by their weights. The view options set the Colors layout (cells, or a list of rows with each color's name or HSL values), its groups (auto hue groups by lightness, the collection's groups, or none) and the cell size, which reflows the cells inside a box that only the density resizes; the list takes focus as one control with grid arrows, typeahead and Enter or Space; cells follow the size and the density of their region, and the chosen cell is marked by a white and a black line along its edge. Collections are JSON documents with colors, groups and `palettes` (`parseColorCollection`, `colorCollectionSchema`); `@tweakpad/ui/color-palette` ships the model with a default and a reduced collection, and every other collection is its own entry, `@tweakpad/ui/color-palette/collections/<id>` (CSS named colors to start). The Color picker's swatches view now composes the palette and takes `collections`; the schemes view and the `schemes` property are removed, and the recent strip draws gapless swatches with one edge.
